      🌍 Geopolitics & Policy

      US Escalates Iran Conflict: Strikes, Sanctions, and Strait of Hormuz Risk

      The US launched airstrikes targeting over 80 Iranian sites and revoked a waiver permitting global Iranian oil sales, escalating tensions after repeated tanker attacks in the Strait of Hormuz. The Treasury's decision removes a 60-day grace period, stranding tens of millions of barrels already at sea. Iran has threatened "decisive measures" in response, raising the risk of renewed supply disruption and commodity price volatility. This represents the sharpest military-economic pressure on Tehran since early 2024.
      Source: Bloomberg, FT, BBC

      NATO Allies Commit £37bn to Collaborative Missile Program

      NATO members announced a £37 billion financing commitment for a new multilateral missile initiative, with UK PM Keir Starmer convening over a dozen leaders at the NATO summit in Ankara. The project signals sustained European defense spending despite fiscal constraints and underpins Trump administration pressure on alliance members to meet 2% GDP targets. Procurement cycles will span 8–12 years, anchoring industrial demand.
      Source: BBC

      Marine Le Pen Launches 2027 Presidential Bid Despite Court Conviction

      Hours after France's appellate court upheld her conviction for misuse of public funds, far-right National Rally leader Marine Le Pen announced her candidacy for the 2027 presidential election. She frames the ruling as judicial overreach, mirroring populist challenges to institutional checks across Europe and the US. Polling and regulatory scrutiny of her campaign finances will be key indicators for European political fragmentation heading into late 2026.
      Source: Le Monde, BBC

      Australia Faces New Pressure from China's Pacific Military Posture

      Following China's recent ballistic missile test, Australia is accelerating treaty and alliance architecture across the Pacific to counter Beijing's military foothold expansion. Canberra's counterweight strategy includes deepening relationships with island nations and expanding defense commitments. The contest mirrors broader geopolitical realignment in Indo-Pacific supply chains and semiconductor positioning.
      Source: NYT

      🇫🇷 🇷 French/European Entrepreneurship

      Proxima Fusion Closes €411M: Europe's Largest Fusion Investment

      Munich-based Proxima Fusion announced a €411 million ($468 million) Series round led by XTX Ventures and East X Ventures, with RWE participation, valuing the company at €2.4 billion. The raise is the largest European fusion financing to date and positions Proxima as a tier-1 alternative energy player competing with US incumbents. Google's indirect backing (via XTX ecosystem) signals accelerating institutional momentum in European deeptech.
      Source: EU Startups, Sifted

      France's €25B AI Strategy Unveiled at Station F

      Prime Minister Bruno Retailleau presented a €25 billion government commitment to French AI development at Station F, framing it as a counterweight to US and Chinese innovation velocity. The plan targets compute infrastructure, talent retention, and regulatory clarity for EU Model Act alignment. Polytechnique Ventures simultaneously committed to allocating 5% of its latest fund to pre-seed AI startups, signaling capital concentration at seed and mega-rounds.
      Source: Maddyness

      BIZAY (Lisbon) Raises €48.75M Series D; Eyes Profitability

      Lisbon-based mass customization platform BIZAY closed a €48.75 million ($55M) Series D round led by existing investor Indico Capital Partners, targeting €88.6 million revenue and profitability in 2026. The raise accelerates US expansion and consolidation in the print-on-demand ecosystem. This represents successful European founder-led exit financing without US-anchored lead syndication.
      Source: EU Startups, Tech.eu

      Luffy AI (UK) Raises €9.4M Series A for Neuroplastic Real-Time Control

      Oxfordshire-based Luffy AI secured €9.4 million (£8.1 million) in Series A funding led by BGF to scale neuroplastic AI for industrial adaptive control systems. The investment validates emerging British strength in applied AI for manufacturing, competing against Siemens and ABB incumbents. Existing investors joined, signaling strong founder-investor alignment.
      Source: EU Startups, Tech.eu

      🔍 Signal of the Day

      Why Europe's Deeptech Capital Ceiling Is Rising (And What It Means for Your Portfolio)

      The €411M Proxima Fusion round is not an outlier—it's a structural inflection point. Over the past 18 months, European venture has migrated from chasing Series A velocity (2020–2023 playbook) toward Series D+ consolidation, strategic corporate partnerships, and cross-border infrastructure plays. Polytechnique's pre-seed allocation, France's €25B AI commitment, and Retailleau's regulatory clarity signals have created a three-layer ecosystem: (1) mega-rounds (€300M+) in fusion, deep learning, and quantum; (2) mid-market Series B-C (€15–80M) in applied AI, biotech, and climate; (3) pre-seed scarcity, forcing founders to seek US accelerators or corporate CVC.

      This rebalancing reflects three drivers: first, European LPs are increasingly willing to hold conviction longer (lower J-curve pressure); second, regulatory clarity around AI and green tech has shifted capital from consumer tech toward infrastructure and hard science; third, geopolitical fragmentation (US-China decoupling, NATO rearmament) has made European supply-chain sovereignty a strategic asset. For a 21-year-old Polytechnique student with SP500/NASDAQ ETF exposure, this means: European founders are now competing with US VCs on infrastructure and deep science, not consumer velocity. Your portfolio (TNO, ETZ, AWAT) benefits from European capex cycles in defense, quantum, and chip manufacturing, but watch for overvaluation in mid-market SaaS exits—European founders are increasingly repricing toward 8–12x revenue multiples, not 12–18x.
