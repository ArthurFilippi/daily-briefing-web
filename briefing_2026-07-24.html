    🔍 Signal of the Day

    The Bifurcation of AI Capex: Proven Winners vs. Structural Anxiety

    Intel's 25% revenue growth and forward guidance beat, coupled with Nvidia's $1.5B supply-chain commitment and Anthropic's valuation spike to $124B, paint a picture of a thriving AI infrastructure arms race. Yet simultaneously, tech bonds are selling off on "AI debt fears," Google and Tesla equities plunged, and Korean chipmakers are experiencing violent volatility. What explains the paradox?

    The answer lies in a two-tier market: (1) companies with demonstrable AI revenue streams and pricing power (Intel, Nvidia, enterprise software vendors like SAP) are capturing genuine value; (2) everyone else—including legacy consumer-tech giants and startups betting on speculative AI monetization—faces a credibility crisis. Investors are asking a brutal question: when does capex actually convert to earnings?

    For an early-stage founder, this bifurcation is a warning and an opportunity. The warning: hype alone no longer sustains venture funding or equity
