The commitment signals confidence in French energy resilience despite Middle East supply tensions. Source: Le Monde, TechCrunch

Leading business groups issued joint statements condemning the government's decision to freeze payroll tax relief (allègements) while raising the SMIC by 2.4% effective June 1. They termed it a "double penalty at the worst moment," signaling labor cost pressures will constrain hiring and startup scaling this quarter. Source: Le Monde

🔍 Signal of the Day

Infrastructure as Geopolitical Asset: SoftBank's €75bn Bet Redefines AI Hierarchy

The fact: SoftBank commits €75 billion to French AI datacenters (5 GW by 2031)—the largest European infrastructure investment of its kind—while OpenRouter raises $113M and Accenture acquires Ookla, signaling capital rotation from software toward physical compute layers.

Broader pattern: AI infrastructure (power, cooling, land, water, fiber) is becoming scarce and strategically controlled. Unlike software, datacenters cannot be instantly replicated or moved; governments now treat them as critical infrastructure equivalent to energy and telecom. France's stable grid, regulatory clarity, and EU subsidies (IPCEI, Green Deal) make it attractive versus UK (post-Brexit complexity) and Germany (energy volatility). Simultaneously, Chinese industrial investments in Morocco and Asian supply-chain bets show Beijing is hedging against Western decoupling by building regional compute hubs.

Implication for a future entrepreneur: The golden age of pure software startups competing globally is closing. Founders building AI applications (regardless of vertical) will soon need to own or contract deeply for compute—either through hyperscaler partnerships (expensive, vendor lock-in) or by co-locating in emerging regional hubs. The next 5 years will see winner-take-most dynamics in infrastructure layer (AWS, Azure, SoftBank, state-backed EU players), which means marginal VC-backed software plays face brutal unit economics. The real moat shifts to vertical integration (e.g., biotech AI + compute + IP) or geographic arbitrage (build in France, sell globally with EU compliance baked in). Plan capital raises accordingly—infrastructure-adjacent founders will outcompete pure-play AI app teams.
