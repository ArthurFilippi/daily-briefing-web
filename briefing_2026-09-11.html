    🌍 Geopolitics & Policy

    Post-9/11 Wars Cost US $8 Trillion; Strategic Implications Resurface

    The combined cost of US military operations against terrorism since 2001 reached $8 trillion—a figure that dwarfs initial estimates and constrains fiscal space for technology and infrastructure investment. This reckonings occurs as BRICS leaders convene in New Delhi, where China and India compete for alignment on alternatives to Western-led institutions, and as jihadism persists through franchised networks (Al-Qaeda's Sahel/Somalia operations, ISIS's mass-scale ideological exports since 2014). The gap between Cold War-era deterrence models and 21st-century asymmetric threat structures continues to drain budgets from preventive technology and cyber defense. Source: Le Grand Continent

    Houthis Capture Mokha; Iran Tightens Red Sea and Hormuz Control

    Iran-backed Houthi forces seized the strategic Mokha port on September 10, strengthening their position over Red Sea shipping and bringing Tehran closer to effective dominance of two critical global chokepoints. The advance follows months of attacks on commercial vessels and coincides with elevated energy prices, compelling oil markets and central banks to reassess geopolitical risk premiums. Regional states (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Oman) are now pursuing temporary shipping agreements to mitigate disruption. Source: Le Monde

    Hong Kong Sentences Three Tiananmen Vigil Activists to 5+ Years

    Lee Cheuk-yan, Chow Hang-tung, and Albert Ho—organizers of annual remembrance vigils for the 1989 Tiananmen crackdown—were sentenced to between five and seven years imprisonment on subversion charges. The convictions, following 2021 detention, signal Beijing's continued enforcement of security laws that suppress political dissent and accelerate capital flight from Hong Kong's institutional spaces. Source: Le Monde

    Anthropic Reports AI Safety Blocks Against Bioweapon Research

    Anthropic disclosed that its latest large language models now incorporate strengthened safety measures to prevent misuse in biological weapons research and other dual-use scenarios. The disclosure reflects growing regulatory scrutiny of frontier AI systems and signals competitive pressure for safety-first positioning as governments draft AI liability frameworks. Source: Le Monde

    🇫🇷 🇷 French/European Entrepreneurship

    Arlequin AI Secures €28M Series A; European Palantir Ambitions Advance

    Paris-based Arlequin AI closed a €28M Series A round (redalpine, OTB Ventures, Bpifrance Defense Innovation Fund) to scale its topological neural network architecture for large-scale data analysis. Founded by political scientist Hugo Micheron and ex-CNRS engineer Antoine Jardin, the company aims to build a European alternative to Palantir's analytical platforms. The round follows a €4.4M seed in 2025 and positions Arlequin within the "strategic autonomy" wave reshaping French/EU tech investment. Source: FrenchWeb

    Greenly Acquires Swedish Carbon Accounting Rival; Global Scale Accelerates

    Greenly (Paris-based carbon accounting platform) acquired an unnamed Swedish competitor to consolidate European carbon accounting leadership and establish itself as a global player in ESG compliance software. The move reflects consolidation momentum in climate-tech SaaS and positions Greenly against competitors like Persefoni and Watershed in a market driven by regulatory compliance (EU Taxonomy, CSRD, SEC climate disclosure rules). Source: Maddyness

    Ed.ai Raises €5M for AI-Powered Student Assessment Platform

    Lyon-based Ed.ai closed a €5M round (Bpifrance, La Poste Ventures, 50 Partners) to scale an AI system that analyzes handwritten exam copies, identifies individual and cohort learning gaps, and recommends personalized remediation activities to teachers. The platform targets the €2B+ EdTech market and complements France's digital education initiatives. Source: FrenchWeb

    EIF Commits €30M to Nordic Foodtech VC Fund II; Agrifood Deeptech Gains Traction

    The European Investment Fund allocated up to €30M to Finnish venture fund Nordic Foodtech VC's Fund II (target size €80M) to support early-stage agrifood deeptech companies across Scandinavia. The backing underscores EU focus on food security, sustainable agriculture, and resilient supply chains in response to climate and geopolitical volatility. Source: Tech.eu

    💼 Business & Finance

    US 10-Year Treasury Yields Approach 5%; Bond Selloff Intensifies

    Global bond markets faced a broad selloff as US 10-year Treasury yields pushed toward 5%—a psychologically significant threshold—amid structural inflation concerns and geopolitical oil-price pressures (Houthi escalation). Economists project yields could exceed 5% if the Federal Reserve maintains elevated rates through late 2026, constraining equity valuations and venture funding multiples. Treasury Secretary Bessent's $6B bond-operation intervention proved insufficient to stem the move. Source: FT

    Iron Ore Falls to Weekly Loss; Steel Mill Margins Deteriorate

    Iron ore prices declined sharply, posting their steepest weekly loss since June as hot metal output remained seasonally weak and steel mill margins compressed. The move signals softening Chinese demand and adds downside pressure to commodity-linked equities in portfolios. Source: Bloomberg

    China Halts New Battery Factory Construction; Capacity Review Underway

    Chinese authorities suspended new battery and energy-storage factory projects pending a year-end capacity review, signaling concern over overcapacity and margin compression in a sector facing subdued demand. The freeze directly impacts lithium, cobalt supply dynamics and EV/storage equipment manufacturers globally. Source: Bloomberg

    Japan's Producer Prices Rise Faster Than Expected; BOJ Rate-Hike Support Strengthens

    Japan's corporate goods prices accelerated in August, exceeding forecasts and reinforcing the Bank of Japan's case for continued rate increases to combat persistent inflation. BoJ's 2026 normalization trajectory contrasts sharply with potential Fed cuts, creating yen volatility and carry-trade unwinding risks. Source: Bloomberg

    🔍 Signal of the Day

    Defense Spending Recount Meets Venture Capital Scarcity: The $8T Reckoning Reshapes Tech Priorities

    The revelation that post-9/11 US military campaigns cost $8 trillion—versus pre-2001 estimates of $1–2 trillion—collides with a structural shortage of early-stage funding across Europe. While mega-rounds dominate headlines (Bending Spoons acquiring Miro for €1.7B), Antler research shows early-stage European funding is collapsing. This creates a paradox: the US spent two decades fighting asymmetric threats while underfunding the infrastructure needed to prevent them. Now, with fiscal reckonings visible and geopolitical complexity deepening (Houthis tightening Red Sea control, Iran maneuvering), Western governments are pivoting toward strategic autonomy in critical technologies—deeptech, AI, semiconductors, agrifood resilience.

    Arlequin AI's €28M raise from France's Defense Innovation Fund is a case study in this shift. Rather than hoping venture returns will emerge organically, public actors are directly co-investing alongside tier-1 VCs to ensure Europe builds alternatives to US-dominated platforms (Palantir, Google, Nvidia). Simultaneously, EIF is channeling €30M into Nordic Foodtech VC, betting that food security and agricultural resilience are now geopolitical necessities, not venture-return gambles.

    For a founder: This signals a decisive reweighting of capital toward dual-use (defense + commercial) applications. If your deeptech company can credibly serve climate, food security, or state resilience and build a venture-scale business, 2026–2028 is the window to capture public co-investment before geopolitical urgency forces direct state ownership or procurement. Conversely, if your business is purely commercial-return focused, expect compressed valuations and longer fundraising cycles as LPs and GPs re-risk allocations toward "strategic" bets.
