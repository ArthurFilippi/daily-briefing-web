      🌍 Geopolitics & Policy

      GLM-5.2: Chinese Model Surpasses OpenAI, Anthropic Latest

      Zhipu AI released GLM-5.2, demonstrating superior performance against recent iterations from Anthropic and OpenAI. This marks a potential inflection: not mere parity, but measurable lead in reasoning tasks. The timing coincides with US policy pivoting toward quantum and post-quantum infrastructure—suggesting Washington acknowledges losing near-term model supremacy and is locking in asymmetric advantages upstream (chip design, cryptography standards). Source: Le Grand Continent

      Iran Claims Strait of Hormuz Control Post-Conflict

      Iranian negotiator Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf stated that post-conflict administration of the Strait of Hormuz "will never return to what it was before." This signals Iran extracting geopolitical rent from ongoing Middle East negotiations. Aluminum fell three months to lows as markets priced in resumed supply flows—a rare moment where commodity markets offered hard truth about peace odds. Source: Le Monde

      Trump Administration Escalates Quantum, Post-Quantum Security Push

      Two executive orders signed to accelerate quantum computing R&D and secure data infrastructure against future quantum decryption. The move acknowledges that harvesting encrypted data now for decryption later is an active threat. This is not speculative—it's core to the US strategic pivot away from short-term AI dominance toward long-term infrastructure lock-in. Source: DW

      Post-Reflecting Pool: $14.7M Repaint Already Peeling

      Trump's freshly painted "American flag blue" Reflecting Pool is already losing paint integrity, with algae blooming in deteriorating water. The administration threatened ten-year prison terms for vandalism. This is simultaneously comic and indicative: massive capex deployed on symbolic infrastructure, accountability mechanisms rigid, but underlying material science and maintenance budgets inadequate. Source: DW

      Paris-Boston clinical-stage biopharma launched with €143M ($165M) oversubscribed Series A, co-led by Jeito Capital. This is the largest Series A in French biotech history, targeting inflammatory disease biologics. Signal: LP appetite for European deeptech remains robust despite awareness that infrastructure dependency (compute, regulatory arbitrage) remains skewed toward US/China. Source: EU-Startups

      Last-mile delivery platform Grid.online secured €4M after scaling parcel volumes over 10× in one year, surpassing 1M deliveries through shared network. Growth in logistics infrastructure—unglamorous but defensible—remains a European winner. Source: Tech.eu

      🔍 Signal of the Day

      Chinese AI Models + US Policy Pivot = Margin Compression Ahead

      The fact: GLM-5.2 outperforms Anthropic Claude and OpenAI's latest on reasoning benchmarks. Simultaneously, Trump administration signs quantum EOs and funds post-quantum cryptography. Iran's negotiator claims Strait control. SpaceX shares fall 3 days running.

      Broader pattern: The US is no longer betting on winning the AI model race on merit; it's locking in infrastructure asymmetry (quantum, chip design, cryptography standards, satellite compute) as the next battleground. China is proving that efficiency in training + novel architectures can catch scale-obsessed Western labs. Europe is raising record capital for deeptech but admitting—via public discourse on "controlling the AI stack"—that it may remain a consumer of US/China infrastructure.

      For a 21-year-old entrepreneur: If you're building an AI application, your moat is not the model—it's either (a) proprietary data (healthcare, supply chain, manufacturing), (b) deployment speed in a hard vertical, or (c) infrastructure play (quantum-safe encryption, distributed compute, chip tooling). Founding a general-purpose LLM company in 2026 is capital-intensive arbitrage, not innovation. Deeptech (biotech, space, energy, quantum) capital exists in Europe, but expect US/China to control the base layer for 5+ years. Build leverage to that layer or own the data/customer relationship.
