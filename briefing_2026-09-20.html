      🌍 Geopolitics & Policy

      Germany's AfD Poised for Regional Election Gains

      Two Länder elections held today in Berlin and Mecklenburg-Vorpommern will test whether the far-right Alternative for Germany can sustain momentum. The party's rise reflects organizational capacity under strategist Götz Kubitschek, who has coordinated a cultural-conquest playbook across media and grassroots networks. Strong results could destabilize Chancellor Friedrich Merz's coalition arithmetic, forcing CDU/CSU into complex negotiations or minority government—a first for postwar Germany at federal level. Source: Le Grand Continent

      Myanmar's UN Envoy Defies Military Junta After Five Years

      U Kyaw Moe Tun has maintained control of his New York diplomatic post and Upper East Side townhouse since refusing to recognize Myanmar's 2021 military coup. His defiance—backed by ASEAN and Western capitals—represents rare continuity in a state fractured between military rule and shadow governance. The standoff underscores how diplomatic real estate and institutional presence can outlast de facto power. Source: New York Times

      Asia-Pacific Allies Decouple Defense Strategy From US Umbrellas

      Trump's unpredictable signaling on regional security has triggered accelerated arms buildups and bilateral defense pacts among Japan, South Korea, Australia, and Vietnam. Allies now perceive Washington's commitment as contingent rather than standing—forcing them to invest in autonomous strike capacity and economic resilience independent of US guarantees. This structural shift will persist regardless of US administration changes. Source: Le Monde

      Macron Reasserts French Sovereignty Over North Atlantic Territories

      The French president arrived in Saint-Pierre-et-Miquelon Saturday to reaffirm control of the archipelago in response to Trump's earlier territorial interest. He will meet Canadian PM Mark Carney, signaling Franco-Canadian coordination against US expansionism and deepening Ottawa's distance from Washington. The visit hardwires French-Canadian defense alignment on maritime borders and resource access. Source: Le Monde

French energy diplomacy gains leverage in South America. Source: Bloomberg

      Australia's Residential Real Estate Market Cools Amid Rate-Hike Signals

      Auction clearance rates in Australian property fell this week as the Reserve Bank approaches a decision on interest rate increases, with concerns of hikes intensifying. Subdued sentiment reflects household exposure to debt and mortgage stress—a bellwether for consumption weakness in H4 2026. Source: Bloomberg

      Warren Buffett Steps Down as Berkshire Hathaway Chairman

      The 96-year-old philanthropist and investor relinquished the chairman role after nearly six decades building Berkshire into a $900B+ conglomerate, citing "Father Time always wins." He remains on the board as chairman emeritus and continues as CEO. The move signals succession planning and signals stability rather than crisis. Source: New York Times

      🔍 Signal of the Day

      German Stability on the Ballot: Why Today Matters Beyond Regional Elections

      Berlin and Mecklenburg-Vorpommern hold state elections today with the AfD expected to advance, testing whether Merz's CDU/CSU can hold coalition ground. The pattern is structural: far-right parties exploit cultural anxiety via organized messaging (Kubitschek's playbook), while centrist coalitions fragment across immigration, energy, and sovereignty issues. If the AfD surpasses 25% in either state, it signals not just electoral success but operational capacity to govern or veto—moving the party from protest to power.

      For a European entrepreneur, this matters because regulatory certainty and capital availability both depend on political stability. A weakened Merz government facing AfD-enabled paralysis on climate investment, AI regulation, or defense procurement creates a 12–24-month window of policy vacuum. This is when nimble deeptech founders in quantum, battery chemistry, or autonomous systems can exploit regulatory delays or secure grandfathered exemptions—but it also means VC dry powder waits on clarity. The broader pattern: European political fracture = entrepreneurial risk premium + longer fundraising timelines.

      Watch for post-election coalition negotiations. If Merz cannot form a majority without Greens or SPD, expect 6+ weeks of gridlock and delayed €50B+ industrial policy commitments.
