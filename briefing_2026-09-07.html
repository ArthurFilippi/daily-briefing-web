    🌍 Geopolitics & Policy

    Germany's AfD Secures Historic 44% in Saxony-Anhalt, But Faces Coalition Deadlock

    The Alternative for Germany (AfD) dominated the eastern state election with its strongest-ever result, capturing 44% of votes while the ruling CDU collapsed to 17%. The party, founded in 2013, fell short of an absolute majority and lacks a ready coalition partner—no mainstream party will govern with it, and forming a state government remains unclear. This outcome signals the far-right's transition from protest movement to serious contender, raising questions about coalition viability across Germany ahead of national elections. Source: DW, BBC, NYT

    EU Counters Trump with Investment in Greenland Amid Strategic Pressure

    Ursula von der Leyen visited Greenland to announce major EU investment and political deepening in response to Donald Trump's demands for greater US territorial control over the mineral-rich Arctic territory. The move reflects European anxiety over strategic vulnerability and resource competition. Denmark's Arctic possession sits atop critical rare-earth and energy reserves, making it a focal point for great-power contestation. Source: DW

    Forest Megafires Now Kill ≥100,000 Annually; Damage "Vastly Underestimated"

    The World Meteorological Organization attributes at least 100,000 deaths per year to toxic smoke from extreme wildfires, which have tripled globally since 1990. Beyond casualty counts, peatland burns (including Borneo's "carbon bombs") release ancient carbon stores and blanket South East Asia in carcinogenic haze. Volunteers in Indonesia risk their lives defusing these fires, yet the health toll remains largely unaccounted for in policy frameworks. Source: Le Monde, BBC

    Indonesia's Microplastics Crisis Moves into Air and Rainfall

    Microplastics, traditionally absorbed through food and water, now contaminate Jakarta's air and rain, marking a systemic shift in exposure pathways. New York Times correspondent Sui-Lee Wee investigated how particles enter the atmosphere, linking industrial processes and coastal degradation to this emerging threat. Source: NYT

    Austria Enforces Islamic Headscarf Ban for Under-14s as Schools Reopen

    Austria implemented a ban on headscarves for primary and lower-secondary students, framing it as protection of girls' rights. Critics argue the law fuels anti-Muslim sentiment and raises religious freedom concerns. Source: BBC

    US Envoys Witkoff and Kushner Meet Zelenskyy After Putin Talks; No Breakthrough

    Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner held talks in Kyiv with President Volodymyr Zelenskyy one day after meetings with Vladimir Putin in Moscow, attempting to revive diplomatic momentum. The shuttle produced no visible progress on ceasefire or territorial terms, signaling that mediation remains stalled. Source: DW, NYT

    Australia Mandates Social Media Algorithm Opt-Out; "Substantial" Penalties for Non-Compliance

    Australian regulation will allow users to disable algorithmic feeds on Meta, Google, and TikTok platforms; firms face major penalties if they refuse. The measure aims to reduce addictive design and algorithmic amplification of harmful content. Source: BBC
