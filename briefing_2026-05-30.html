🌍 Geopolitics & Policy

China's Economic Ascent Reshapes Global Hierarchy

China's growth between 1978 and 2022 exceeded US expansion during the Gilded Age by 290 times and Japan's postwar boom by 20 times—an unprecedented trajectory in recorded history. This economic magnitude compounds technological catch-up concerns in AI, semiconductors, and quantum computing. Source: Le Grand Continent

US Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth stated at Singapore's Shangri-La Dialogue that ties with China have stabilized and no state should dominate Asia, while demanding Asian allies increase defense spending to 3.5% of GDP. The rhetoric marks a pivot from confrontation toward managed competition, though European NATO members were pointedly criticized for underinvestment. Source: DW

🔍 Signal of the Day

The Bifurcation of Geopolitical Tech Advantage

China's economic growth trajectory—290 times larger than the US Gilded Age expansion—demonstrates a scale of resource mobilization that compounds technological catch-up velocity. Simultaneously, the Trump administration is signaling US-China ties have "stabilized," while SpaceX locks $4.16B in government contracts and the White House moves to politicize federal research grants.

The pattern reveals a fundamental restructuring: the US is shifting from broad technological dominance toward concentrated defense-critical systems (space, missiles, AI agents with governance controls), while China's sheer economic mass sustains horizontal advances across oncology, semiconductors, and quantum research. Europe, meanwhile, is consolidating regulatory power (Temu fines, anti-corruption reforms unlocking €16B) but remains capital-constrained relative to both.

For an aspiring deeptech founder: this moment favors specialization in defense-adjacent or regulatory-arbitrage technologies. Pure play infrastructure (compute, data centers) faces margin compression from China's scale; instead, target problems where geopolitical fragmentation increases addressable markets (redundant supply chains, localized AI inference, sovereign quantum). French founders now have a window with US VCs (MokN's GV investment) before capital follows the geopolitical fault lines hardening around AI safety, space systems, and defense.
