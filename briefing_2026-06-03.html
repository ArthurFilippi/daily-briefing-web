🔍 Signal of the Day

The $75B Infrastructure Bet: Why Geopolitics + AI = Record Capital Flows

The fact: SpaceX targets $75B IPO simultaneously as Middle East conflict forces direct US-Iran military engagement, while Megaport raises $594M for AI inference and Applied Aerospace closes $650M round—creating an unprecedented convergence of space, defense, and compute capital.

Broader pattern: This is not cyclical VC enthusiasm. Defense capex spending, semiconductor supply chain reshoring, data center buildout backlogs, and geopolitical fragmentation are now structural drivers of deeptech funding. Unlike 2021-23 when AI hype alone justified valuations, today's mega-rounds rest on hard constraints: physical infrastructure cannot be built overnight, and democracies are willing to fund it. Governments are directly de-risking early-stage ventures through subsidies (EU AI Act exceptions, US defense contracts, CHIPS Act). The S-curve is real.

Implication for an École Poly founder: The next 18 months offer a uniquely compressed timeline. If you have hardware, infrastructure, or dual-use deeptech (quantum, robotics, synthetic biology, advanced materials), now is the moment to raise at scale. Public markets are open, government mandates are accelerating procurement, and geopolitical urgency is removing the "prove it works first" gate that killed 2022-24 deeptech. But the window closes as soon as a China detente or energy crisis stabilizes; founders bootstrapping infrastructure plays in 2027+ will face 60% lower valuations. Move fast on capital structure, regulatory pre-approval, and anchor customers—execution will outweigh pitch.
