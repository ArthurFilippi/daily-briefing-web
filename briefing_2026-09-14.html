      🔍 Signal of the Day

      The AI safety call is a window into venture capital misalignment, not an inflection point

      Anthropic and OpenAI's public warnings about AI development speeds are being treated by markets as demand destruction. SoftBank's 13% selloff presumes these companies will actually decelerate spending. That's almost certainly wrong. What the warnings reveal instead is a structural rift between research teams (who understand capability/safety tradeoffs) and capital allocators (who fund races, not restraint). Anthropic itself is preparing to go public; OpenAI is in fundraising mode. Neither firm can actually slow down without admitting to investors that prior deployment promises were premature. The warnings are performative—safety theater for regulators and the public—while capital continues to flow unabated. For entrepreneurs, the lesson is clearer than ever: in AI infrastructure and model development, capital concentration is accelerating. Only firms that can sustain multi-year cash burns while competing on scale will survive. This favors exits into mega-cap acquirers (SoftBank, Google, Microsoft) over independent growth paths. Smaller deeptech labs with 18-month runways should be closing or consolidating now.
