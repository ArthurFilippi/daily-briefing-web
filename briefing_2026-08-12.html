      🌍 Geopolitics & Policy

      Trump's Unplanned Plane Swap Reveals Iran Escalation Risk

      On July 8 at NATO summit's close, Trump was covertly moved from Air Force One to a second aircraft due to Iranian threat warnings, per Washington Post and NYT reporting. The swap—kept secret from travelling press—underscores operational chaos in presidential security and signals Tehran escalation beyond diplomatic posturing. This feeds broader Strait of Hormuz tension: Pakistan's defence minister said US-Iran are "close to some sort of arrangement," yet both sides simultaneously hardened public stances. For investors, energy volatility persists; Libyan drone attack on Zawiya refinery (largest in country) compounds supply uncertainty. Source: BBC, Le Monde

      Colombia Earthquake: 181 Dead, Rescue Operations Underway

      A 5.x magnitude earthquake struck Cali on August 11, collapsing a five-storey apartment building and killing at least 181 people. Emergency crews and volunteers are racing against time to extract survivors from rubble; reporters on-site documented live rescue of a buried victim. This marks a major humanitarian event in South America's third-largest economy and will disrupt infrastructure/logistics networks. Source: NYT

      Russia Plans Massive Troop Mobilization by Autumn

      Ukrainian President Zelensky, citing internal Russian documents from his intelligence services, disclosed that Moscow will mobilize "several hundred thousand" troops after a simulated parliamentary election in early autumn. The timing suggests preparation for sustained offensive operations as 2026 winter approaches. This is the most concrete evidence yet of Russian escalation intent and signals Ukraine must accelerate Western arms delivery and training cycles. Source: Le Monde

      European Heat Wave Intensifies; France Issues Orange Alert

      Seventy-eight French departments now face orange heat alert (up from 43 Tuesday), with temperatures forecast to exceed 35°C and peaks at 39°C in some zones. Spain's total solar eclipse event Wednesday adds logistical strain: authorities expect 450,000 extra visitors, compounding fire risk in regions ravaged by July megafires. This represents compounding climate/security hazard. Source: Le Monde

      🔍 Signal of the Day

      Physical AI Infrastructure is Becoming the New Venture Capital Gravity Well

      Over the past 72 hours, three structural moves converge: Accel closes $3.5B AI fund (oversubscribed), South Korea commits $1T sovereign wealth to AI/robotics, and CoreWeave reports 2x revenue growth in GPU hosting. Separately, European deeptech capital is flowing to hardware (Vertical Aerospace €86.6M, Yulu $93M e-bikes, Exobiosphere microgravity biotech). The pattern is unmistakable: venture capital is rotating away from software-as-a-service and consumer fintech toward the physical substrate of the AI era—compute infrastructure, power delivery, robotic logistics, and biotech that requires hardware deployment.

      This matters because it reshapes where founders win. A 22-year-old building yet another AI chatbot fine-tuned on proprietary data faces compressed margins and crowded competition. A 22-year-old building timing synchronization for data centers (SiTime's business), grid-scale power electronics (Vertiv's play), or microgravity drug discovery, operates in regulatory/capex moats that VC and sovereigns will fund at 3-5x historical valuations. The bottleneck has moved from software deployment to atoms: silicon wafers, real estate for data centers, spectrum, and physical supply chains.

      For the École Polytechnique engineer with deeptech ambitions: the window to launch hard tech (energy, space, biotech, industrial robotics) is now. Capital is abundant, regulatory support is becoming bipartisan geopolitical priority, and margins are structural rather than algorithmic. Consumer software founders, meanwhile, face a 2-3 year funding desert.
