      🔍 Signal of the Day

      When AI Capex Meets Higher Rates: The Debt Spiral Question

      Singapore's STT GDC borrowing $2.4B for data center expansion while Kioxia warns of demand normalization and Citigroup slashes battery demand forecasts reveals a fault line in the AI boom narrative. The structural issue: infrastructure builders committed to hyper-scale capex in a rising-rate environment, betting that utilization will justify debt servicing costs. Recent "stumbles" (OpenAI delays, Anthropic safety concerns, missed generalization targets) are eroding the certainty that once justified blank-check capex.

      The pattern mirrors 2021-22 crypto mining craze—massive infrastructure commitment, then demand deceleration forces asset write-downs. Here, the leverage is institutional (pension funds, SWFs financing data centers) rather than retail, but the risk architecture is identical: sunk costs trap operators into marginal-margin operation to cover financing charges.

      For an aspiring deeptech founder, this signals a near-term window of opportunity: capital flowing toward "proven" infrastructure plays (data centers, cooling systems, chip design) precisely when cost-of-capex risk is becoming visible. Venture money will rotate from generalist AI plays toward infrastructure-layer picks offering concrete ROI, not speculative scaling. Position your pitch accordingly.
