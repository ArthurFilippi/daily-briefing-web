    FBI Dismantles $400M North Korean Crypto Scam Using Deepfake Video Meetings

The attack underscores convergence of AI-generated fraud and financial crime at scale—a threshold that redefines cyber threat modeling for institutions holding digital assets. Source: DW

    French Cybersecurity Regulation NIS 2 Delayed Two Years; Assembly Vote Imminent

    France was obligated to transpose the EU's Network and Information Security Directive 2 (NIS 2) by October 2024. The bill only reached the National Assembly in November 2026—a 24-month delay blamed on parliamentary dissolution, political instability and deadlock over encryption standards. The holdup has left 50,000+ French firms in regulatory limbo and created compliance uncertainty for startups in critical infrastructure. Once passed, NIS 2 will establish mandatory incident reporting and supply-chain security audits, reshaping the operating environment for deeptech and cloud vendors. Source: FrenchWeb

Source: Bloomberg

    🔍 Signal of the Day

    When Titans Fall: Why the Huang Departure Matters Beyond Nvidia

    The fact. Jensen Huang, who built Nvidia from a startup into the world's most valuable company over 33 years, resigned Thursday as CEO following disclosed affairs—one allegedly with a Chinese intelligence operative—and a simultaneous Congressional summons. His exit was unconditional; the board moved quickly to install CFO Colette Kress as interim CEO and Harvey Jones as chair. Retail investors responded with record buying volume, not euphoria about succession, but pure hope that the leadership crisis won't metastasize.

    Broader pattern. The Huang departure crystallizes a structural tension in modern tech leadership: founder-led companies trading on visionary momentum often lack institutional safeguards that catch governance crises before they explode publicly. Nvidia had no heir apparent; its board relied on founder charisma as a substitute for deep operational redundancy. The timing—coinciding with Microsoft's Outlook outage and North Korean deepfake-driven crypto theft—reveals that when one critical figure or system fails, confidence cascades downward across the entire stack. Capital doesn't flee the AI sector; it hunts for alternative technical bets and more distributed governance models.

    Implication for entrepreneurs. If you are building a deeptech or infrastructure company with venture backing, take note: boards and LPs are now actively scanning for concentration risk in leadership and operations. The Huang moment will accelerate demand for COOs with P&L accountability, documented succession plans and board diversity that isn't decorative. Founders who conflate personal brand with company resilience will face harder questions during governance conversations. Build redundancy into your narrative now, or face it imposed on you under duress later.
